Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 September 2026

Traxplore is an app for marking the countries and cities you have visited on a globe. This page explains what information is collected while you use it, why, where it is stored and what rights you have over it.

Data controller: Cihan Güzelsoy
Contact: cihanguzelsoy@gmail.com

Write to this address about anything on this page, including data deletion requests.

1. Information collected

To create your account

  • Email address — so you can sign in and reset your password.
  • Password — stored hashed. We cannot see the plain text either.
  • Username — required, chosen once, cannot be changed later. Visible to anyone who searches for you in the app.

What you enter yourself (all optional)

  • Name — shown on your profile.
  • Location text — free text such as "Ankara, Türkiye". This is a sentence you typed, not a measured location.
  • Profile photo — a silhouette is shown if you don't add one.
  • Countries and cities you have visited.
  • Month and year of a visit — if you choose to add it. The day and time are never asked for and never stored. This is deliberate: a day and time would be a location record rather than a memory.
  • Your city notes and ratings — nobody but you can see these.
  • City photos — photos you upload are private by default; only you can see them unless you make a photo public one by one.

What the app records on its own

  • Friendships — whose requests you accepted.
  • Users you have blocked.
  • Reports — if you report a user or a photo: who reported, what was reported, the reason chosen and your optional description.
  • Creation and update timestamps.

2. What is not collected

Stated explicitly, because most apps do collect these:

  • No device location. No GPS, no IP-based location guessing. Everything on the map is what you marked by hand.
  • No advertising ID, trackers or analytics. No Google Analytics or similar tool is used.
  • No cookies. What keeps you signed in is stored by your browser on your own device (localStorage) and is not sent to us.
  • No access to your contacts, photo library or other apps. Only the photo you actively choose to upload is sent.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with anyone for advertising.

3. Who can see what

Traxplore has no direct messaging and none is planned. Visibility is under your control through two switches:

InformationWho can see it
Your username, profile photo, name, location text, country/city counts Anyone signed in to the app
Your map and the photos you made public Everyone if your profile is open; only your friends if it is closed
Your travel log (which city, which month and year) Only if you turned the travel log switch on, and then only to whoever your profile setting allows. It is off by default; while off, not even your friends can see it
Your notes and ratingsOnly you
Your private photos Only you. (If a photo is reported, the administrator may review it.)
Your email address Only you and the administrator. It is never shown to other users

These rules are enforced by the database itself, not by hiding things in the interface. Nobody can reach data they should not see by going around the app.

4. Where your data is stored

Application data is stored on Supabase infrastructure. The servers are located in Frankfurt, Germany (AWS eu-central-1 region). Supabase Inc., the provider, is a company based in the United States, but the physical location of the data is Frankfurt. The website is published on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. — Microsoft).

International transfer

Because the servers are located outside Türkiye, your data is transferred abroad. This transfer is necessary for the app to work at all: without your account, map and photos being on those servers there is no app. By creating an account you give explicit consent to this transfer (Turkish DPL art. 9). If you wish to withdraw that consent, deleting your account is enough — deletion is built into the app and removes your data from those servers.

Your data stays within the European Union. It does not leave the scope of the European data protection regime (GDPR) and is not moved to the United States or any other country.

5. How long data is kept

  • While your account exists, the information above is kept.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, name, location text, profile photo, visited cities, notes, ratings, dates, uploaded photos (both the records and the files), friendships, blocks and login account are deleted.
  • Reports are kept for 12 months and then deleted automatically. See below.
  • A deleted username is never issued again, so that "@example" in an old record is never confused with a new "@example".

Why reports survive account deletion

Deleting your account cannot also be the way to erase reports made about you; if it were, someone who harasses others could reset their history by deleting and re-registering each time. Reports are therefore kept for 12 months. What is kept is minimal: the username, the reason and the text of the report. The link to the account identity is severed — no email, name, location, map or photos remain. The legal basis for this retention is the legitimate interest in preventing abuse (Turkish DPL art. 5/2-f, GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: Profile > Delete My Account. The action cannot be undone and you are asked to type your username to confirm. What is deleted and what is kept is also written in the confirmation dialog.

7. Your rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (art. 11) and the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your data is processed and request information about it
  • Learn the purpose of processing
  • Know the parties to whom it is transferred, at home or abroad
  • Request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate
  • Request erasure or destruction
  • Object to processing
  • Request a copy of your data (portability)

To exercise these rights, write to cihanguzelsoy@gmail.com. You will receive a reply within 30 days at the latest.

8. Security

Connections are encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed and never kept in plain text anywhere. Who may see what is defined at the database level. Even so, it should be said honestly that no system is 100% secure.

9. Age limit

Traxplore is not intended for anyone under 16. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, the account and its data are deleted. A parent who believes their child has created an account can write to the address above.

10. Changes to this policy

If this text changes, this page is updated and the date at the top is renewed. Significant changes are also announced inside the app.
