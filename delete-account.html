Deleting your account and data

Traxplore — developer: Cihan Güzelsoy · Last updated: 18 September 2026

You can delete your Traxplore account and the data attached to it at any time, without asking anyone. There are two ways.

1. From inside the app (recommended)

Deletion happens immediately; there is no waiting period.

  • Open Traxplore and sign in to your account.
  • Tap the profile card in the top right.
  • Open the Settings screen with the gear icon at the top right of your profile.
  • Scroll to the bottom and find Delete my account.
  • Confirm, and the account and the data listed below are deleted.

2. By e-mail

If you cannot sign in, write to cihanguzelsoy@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your account. Once your identity is confirmed, the request is completed within 30 days at the latest.

Data that is deleted

The following is deleted permanently and cannot be restored:

  • Your sign-in account: e-mail address and password
  • Your profile: name, location, username, profile photo
  • Your map: the countries and cities you marked, and the travel dates
  • Your city notes, ratings and public comments
  • The city photos you uploaded (both the records and the files)
  • Your friendships and your block list
  • Your favourites and your "want to go" list

The one thing that is kept: abuse reports

If a report has been filed about you, that record is kept for 12 months after your account is deleted, and is removed automatically at the end of that period.

The reason: deleting and re-creating an account cannot be a way to erase an abuse record. What is kept is therefore minimal — the record contains only your username at that time and the text of the report. Your e-mail address, name, location, photos and map are deleted and do not appear in it.

Usernames: a deleted username is never given to anyone else, so a name in an old report cannot be confused with a different person later.

Deleting data without deleting your account

If you do not want to close your account, you can delete parts of your data from inside the app: unmark cities, delete your photos and comments, or reset your map from the Settings screen. None of this affects your account.
